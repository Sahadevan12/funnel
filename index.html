The Mind Behind True Funnel

Harshit Vora, CA · CFA

Founder & Chief Wealth Strategist

With over 16 years at the intersection of capital markets and corporate finance, Harshit brings a rare vantage point to wealth management — one shaped by decades of structuring IPOs, closing private equity deals, and orchestrating M&A transactions. A qualified Chartered Accountant and CFA, he has spent his career on the other side of the table — raising capital, valuing businesses, and understanding what truly drives long-term value creation.

Before founding True Funnel Financial Services, Harshit led fund-raising mandates across public offerings, institutional placements, and cross-border M&A — working closely with promoters, PE funds, and investment banks. This deep exposure to how capital flows at the institutional level gave him a unique lens on markets that most distributors simply don't have.

He founded True Funnel to bring that institutional rigour to a selective group of ultra-HNI families and business owners — investors who demand more than generic fund recommendations. Every portfolio he builds carries the same diligence he once applied to billion-rupee transactions: thorough, structured, and conviction-driven.
